## The result

Describe the outcome in a few sentences. Distinguish the agent's initial claim from the result that was verified. State unresolved questions.

## Scope and objective

What was tested, under what authorization or lab setup, and what was excluded? Include a public reference where appropriate without exposing private authorization documents.

## What the agent did

Summarize the meaningful steps, decisions, tools and human interventions. Explain the starting context and conditions needed to understand the result.

## Evidence and verification

Link sanitized artifacts in this folder. For each substantive claim, identify the supporting observation and how it was checked. State the tested versions, reproduction conditions and limits. Describe impact only as far as the evidence supports it.

For a demo or benchmark, describe the fixture, measurement method and comparison conditions. For a vulnerability, include a public advisory or disclosure status when available; hold back details that are not ready to publish.

## What worked, what broke

Describe useful behavior, false positives, missed context, sandbox or budget failures, and any manual correction. Report no-finding and failed runs honestly.

## Takeaways and follow-up

What should change in the agent, tools, prompts or workflow? Link a public fix, retest or later entry when available.

## Publication notes

Record who reviewed the entry, the publication date and any remaining redactions. Remove secrets and private target details from both visible content and attachment metadata. Record dated corrections here.
