# Community expectations

Be respectful, specific and constructive. Critique ideas and code rather than people. Do not harass, discriminate, threaten or publish another person's private information.

Keep issues and pull requests focused on the project. Use synthetic examples rather than private target data. Report vulnerabilities through the private route in SECURITY.md.

Maintainers may moderate content and restrict participation to protect the community. Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through GitHub.
